Capital Allocation Tutorial Capital Allocation (ND2) - Wealth Account Rules Based System

ND2 is the second formula in our non discretionary rules based system. It is how we manage our Wealth Account capital to generate Unearned Income (money that works for us: ie; interest, dividends, capital gains, rents, royalties, profits) and build a fortress balance sheet.

Assets on your balance sheet are either making you richer or poorer, adding or decreasing risk, maximizing or minimizing liquidity.

ND2 rules consists of T1 cash and cash-like assets (liquidity), T2 cash flow assets (investments), T3 appreciating assets (speculations).

We recommend an example ratio of 10/70/20 (10% T1 Liquidity, T2 70% Cashflow Assets, 20% T3 Speculations) but you are free to customize it.
